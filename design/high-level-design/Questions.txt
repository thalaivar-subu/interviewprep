❗️Stripe Interview Experience - Position: Staff Platform Engineer

Round 1 – Infrastructure at Global Payments Scale
• How do you design a control plane that survives region-wide database failovers without losing audit trails or payment state?
• Walk through building policy-as-code that actually stops shadow traffic from hitting production ledgers.
• What’s your strategy for rolling out global rate limiting without breaking payment flows during traffic spikes?

Round 2 – Reliability, Scaling & Chaos
• Design an incident response process for a full Stripe outage caused by a misapplied infrastructure change.
• How do you ensure zero-downtime deploys across multiple regions and tenants?
• Explain how you’d use chaos engineering to test payment system resilience.

Round 3 – Security & Compliance
• How do you isolate workloads for different Stripe products while maintaining auditability?
• Walk through your approach to enforcing financial data residency and encryption at rest + in transit.

Round 4 – Leadership & Communication
• How would you explain a complex infra failure to non-technical stakeholders?
• Describe a time you influenced a team decision on platform tooling or architecture.

Final Round – System Design
• Design a multi-tenant, PCI-DSS compliant observability platform for Stripe’s infrastructure.

Get real or get left behind.
